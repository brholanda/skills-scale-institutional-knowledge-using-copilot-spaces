# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and validation across the project lifecycle. They collaborate with developers and product leads to define acceptance criteria and test scenarios, ensuring features meet quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, test plan, and acceptance criteria alignment
- Collaborate with Product and Development on testability and acceptance criteria
- Execute manual and automated testing across feature lifecycle
- Identify quality risks, gaps, and blockers
- Report test coverage metrics and defect trends
- Validate releases and coordinate go/no-go decisions
- Mentor team on testing best practices

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features that meet acceptance criteria
- Minimize post-release defects and production issues
- Provide clear, actionable quality metrics to stakeholders
- Reduce cycle time through effective test automation

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions
- Pull request reviews (quality perspective)
- Daily standups and QA status updates
- Release readiness reviews and deployment validation
- Quality metrics dashboards and trend reporting

### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Review test plans, collaborate on edge cases, review test results
- **Product Managers**: Align on acceptance criteria, define quality thresholds
- **Project Managers**: Escalate quality blockers, report on release readiness
- **Technical Architects**: Discuss testing strategies for complex systems

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide technical guidance on system design, scalability, and integration. They work closely with Product and Development teams to ensure technical feasibility of requirements, alignment with long-term platform vision, and adherence to architectural standards.

### Responsibilities
- Review and guide architectural decisions on new features and integrations
- Assess technical feasibility of product requirements
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers on best practices, design patterns, and code quality standards
- Ensure code quality and adherence to architectural standards
- Plan and manage technical debt reduction initiatives
- Conduct architecture and design reviews

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable, and performant architecture
- Reduce technical debt and prevent architectural drift
- Enable rapid iteration without compromising system integrity
- Minimize future refactoring through good upfront design decisions

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical deep-dives on complex features
- Risk register updates (technical risks)
- Code review participation on critical paths
- Documentation of architectural decisions and trade-offs

### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Mentor on design patterns, review code architecture, guide implementation
- **Product Managers**: Discuss technical feasibility, trade-offs, and roadmap impact
- **Project Managers**: Identify technical dependencies and escalate architectural blockers
- **QA/Testing Leads**: Discuss testing strategies for complex architectures

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads define user experience, visual design, and usability standards. They collaborate with Product and Development teams to ensure features are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with brand standards, ultimately maximizing user adoption and satisfaction.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing to inform design decisions
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and detailed design specifications
- Define accessibility standards and ensure WCAG compliance
- Review implementation against design specifications
- Iterate on designs based on user feedback and testing results
- Establish and maintain design systems and brand guidelines
- Mentor team on UX best practices and accessibility

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences that delight users
- Reduce support burden and confusion from poor UX
- Maximize user adoption, engagement, and satisfaction
- Build consistency across products through design systems

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions with development team
- User research sessions and usability testing reports
- Sprint planning (design story estimation and acceptance)
- Accessibility audits and compliance reviews
- Design documentation and specification handoffs

### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Review implementation, provide design specs, iterate on feedback
- **Product Managers**: Align on user needs, gather research insights, prioritize features
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate design timelines and dependencies
- **QA/Testing Leads**: Define usability test scenarios, validate against acceptance criteria

---

## Executive Sponsor / Stakeholder Lead

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors are senior leaders who champion the project, remove blockers at the executive level, and ensure alignment with organizational strategy. They provide strategic guidance, represent stakeholder interests, and own project governance at the sponsor level.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter, scope, and key decisions
- Remove executive-level blockers and resource constraints
- Provide strategic input on scope, prioritization, and trade-offs
- Champion the project internally and advocate for resources
- Escalate critical risks and business-impacting issues
- Review high-level progress, ROI, and business impact
- Ensure alignment with organizational strategy and priorities
- Represent project to board or senior leadership as needed

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Maintain executive alignment and stakeholder confidence
- Mitigate organizational and strategic risks
- Maximize stakeholder satisfaction and business outcomes
- Enable project success through leadership and resource support

### Typical Communication
- Monthly executive progress updates and dashboards
- Key milestone reviews and decision gates
- Escalation of critical risks and blockers
- Strategic planning sessions and roadmap alignment
- Stakeholder briefings and board-level reporting
- Post-project retrospectives and ROI validation

### Key Interactions
- **Project Managers**: Provide strategic direction, remove escalations, approve decisions
- **Product Managers**: Align on business objectives and strategic priorities
- **Development Team**: Celebrate wins, understand technical constraints at high level
- **Cross-functional Teams**: Coordinate dependencies and resolve inter-team conflicts

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When managing projects, ensure all defined roles are covered and that communication and accountability are clear across role boundaries.
